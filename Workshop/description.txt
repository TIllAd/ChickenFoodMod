[h1]Chicken & Rice[/h1]
A new main dish for PlateUp!: cook chicken, boil a pot of rice and plate them up together.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
[list]
[*]Cook the chicken on a hob or in an oven.
[*]Put rice and water in a pot and cook it. A rice pot gives 5 portions, and the rice sinks as you take them.
[*]Put a rice portion and the chicken on a plate and serve.
[/list]

[h2]Cards[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shredded Chicken[/b] (main variant): some customers want their chicken chopped.
[*][b]Chicken - Broccoli[/b] (topping): chop broccoli, cook it with water in a pot, portion it onto the plate.
[*][b]Lemon Chicken[/b] (topping): add a lemon slice.
[*][b]Chicken - Soy Sauce[/b] (extra): customers may ask for a bottle of soy sauce at the table.
[/list]
Regular side cards work with the chicken plate too. Toppings can be added in any order.

[h2]Details[/h2]
[list]
[*]Colourblind labels for toppings (B = broccoli, Le = lemon, Sh = shredded chicken) and the cooked rice pot (R).
[*]Uses base game and IngredientLib models, so it fits the PlateUp! art style.
[*]Multiplayer: every player needs the mod.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]KitchenLib
[*]IngredientLib
[/list]
